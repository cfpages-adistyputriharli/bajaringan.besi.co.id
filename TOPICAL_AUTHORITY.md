# Topical Authority — bajaringan.besi.co.id

## Role and boundary

BajaRingan.Besi.co.id should become a non-geographic Indonesian reference for cold-formed/light-gauge steel roof and framing systems, with commercial support for verified products and quotations. The safest current role is **independent product-information, quotation, supplier referral, and project-facilitation platform**.

The repository does not prove that the site owner manufactures steel, owns a factory, designs structures, certifies installers, operates a testing laboratory, or has been a distributor since 2012. It also does not substantiate “#1,” “factory price,” universal corrosion resistance, product strength, stock, lead time, service area, or exact prices. Those claims require dated evidence tied to the responsible legal entity and exact product.

Articles explain decision inputs, evidence, interfaces, inspection, and escalation. They do not provide project-specific member sizing, truss geometry, load calculations, fastener schedules, bracing design, connection capacity, fire rating, erection method, or structural acceptance. Those remain with competent designers, verified suppliers/manufacturers, qualified installers, inspectors, and applicable authorities.

## Evidence and editorial standards

1. Cite current official Indonesian requirements, project specifications, primary standards, verified manufacturer data, or qualified engineering review as applicable. Verify edition and scope before naming a clause.
2. Identify whether a statement applies to steel substrate, coating, profile, connection, truss, roof assembly, or whole building.
3. Product dimensions, base/coated thickness, material properties, coating class, mass, capacity, certificates, warranty, price, stock, and lead time require exact-product dated evidence.
4. Structural loads, member sizes, truss spacing, fastener schedules, bracing, anchors, supports, temporary works, erection, fire rating, and repair require qualified project-specific design/approval.
5. Use owned/licensed images and verified case data. Never invent firsthand installation experience, failures, tests, calculations, or project results.
6. K3 content must state competence, permits, stop-work conditions, and emergency interfaces; it cannot normalize working at height or modifying a structure without approval.
7. Climate is addressed by exposure mechanism, not duplicated city pages.
